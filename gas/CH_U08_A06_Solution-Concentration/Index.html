Discussion

When performing many chemical reactions, it is important to measure and know the concentration of solutions that partake in the reaction. This is because the number of moles contained in a sample of solution must be known so the proper amount of solution can be used. Given the required number of moles and the concentration of solution, the needed volume can be calculated.

To prepare a solution of desired concentration requires the use of a mathematical relationship and a mathematical model from the reference tables, shown below:

Mathematical Relationship

1 mole = molar mass (g)

Mathematical Model

n = MV
n = moles
M = Molarity
V = volume

This lab requires the preparation of drink mix with specific concentrations. See the example problem below that explains how to determine the mass of a solute required to make a solution of desired concentration.

How to Prepare a Solution of Desired Concentration

A 250-mL solution of NaOH with a concentration of 2 M is desired. How would this be prepared?

  1. Determine the moles of NaOH needed with the mathematical model n = MV:
    n = MV → n = 2 mol NaOH L
    × 0.250 L
    = 0.5 mol NaOH
  2. Determine the mass of NaOH needed using its molar mass (40 g NaOH/ 1 mole NaOH):
    0.5 mol NaOH
     ×     40 g NaOH1 mol NaOH
     = 20 g NaOH
  3. Place a container with graduations on a scale and tare it. Measure the calculated mass from step 2 into the container.
  4. Add water to the proper volume desired. In this case: 0.250 L, or 250 mL.
  5. Stir the mixture until all the solute is dissolved.

warning Safety Note

This lab allows you to taste the results. This is highly unusual in a chemistry lab. To ensure the safety of all that this entails, make sure to adhere to the following:

  1. Measure all solute in the mixing cup: Tare out the cup and then scoop the proper mass of solute into it.
  2. Never place a spoon on the counter: Always place it down in a designated cup.
  3. Accidental measurement: If you measure too much solute, throw it away. Do not put it back into the container.
  4. Water Source: Only use bottled water to prepare the solution.
