Rounding Explorer

Visualize precision and significant figures

How to use this tool: Enter a measurement (e.g., "0.3542 m" or "3.26E-9 m") with its units in the input box above and click "Display". The number line visualizes where your measurement falls exactly between two rounded values. Numbers exceeding 10 digits or entered in scientific notation are displayed in scientific format. Use the (+) and (-) buttons below to zoom in (increase precision) or zoom out (decrease precision) to see how the number rounds at different levels of significant figures!
