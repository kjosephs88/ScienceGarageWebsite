Lab 18 - Solution Concentration
ANSWER KEY

Part 1: Definitions

1. Define Solute:
The substance that is being dissolved in a solution (e.g., the drink mix).
2. Define Solvent:
The substance that does the dissolving (e.g., the water).
3. Define Solution:
A homogeneous mixture composed of a solute dissolved in a solvent.
4. Define Mole:
A standard scientific unit (6.022 x 10^23) for measuring large quantities of very small entities like atoms or molecules.
5. Define Molar mass:
The mass in grams of one mole of a substance (g/mol).
6. Define Mass:
The amount of matter in an object, measured in grams in this lab.
7. Define Concentration:
The ratio of solute to the total volume of the solution; how 'crowded' the particles are.
8. Define Molarity:
A specific measure of concentration: moles of solute per liter of solution (mol/L).
9. Define Volume:
The amount of space a substance occupies, measured in L or mL.

Part 6: Conclusions

21. Claim: 'Two solutions can contain the same number of moles of solute but have different concentrations'. Support or Refute.
Support. Evidence: Solutions 1 and 2 both have 0.40 moles, but Solution 2 is more concentrated (4M) because it has half the volume (0.10L vs 0.20L) of Solution 1.
22. Claim: 'Two solutions can have the same volume but different concentrations'. Support or Refute.
Support. Evidence: Solutions 3 and 4 both have 0.14 L, but Solution 3 is more concentrated (2M) because it contains more solute mass (10.42g vs 5.21g).
23. Which two solutions tasted the same? Use numerical evidence.
Solutions 1 and 3. Evidence: Both have an identical Molarity of 2 M, and taste is determined by concentration/ratio, not total volume.

Part 7: Post-Lab Questions

24. What is the Molarity of a 2-liter sample containing 28g of KOH? Show your work.
0.25 M.

Work:
28g / 56.1 g/mol = 0.5 mol
0.5 mol / 2 L = 0.25 M
25. Calculate the moles required for 0.50L of 2.0M KNO3? Show your work.
1.0 mol.

Work:
n = M x V = 2.0 M * 0.50 L = 1.0 mol
26. Which is more concentrated: an 8M 200-mL sample or a 4M 500-mL sample? Explain.
The 8 M sample. Explanation: Molarity is the direct measure of concentration; total volume is irrelevant to the intensity of the concentration itself.
27. Explain if it is possible for a solution with a smaller volume and greater solute to be less concentrated.
No, it is mathematically impossible. Increasing the numerator (solute) and decreasing the denominator (volume) will always result in a higher quotient (concentration).
