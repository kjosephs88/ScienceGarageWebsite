2. The volume of a gas is 4.00 liters at 293 K and constant pressure. For the volume of the gas to become 3.00 liters, the Kelvin temperature must be equal to
(1) $$ \frac{3.00 \times 293}{4.00} $$
(3) $$ \frac{3.00 \times 4.00}{293} $$
(2) $$ \frac{4.00 \times 293}{3.00} $$
(4) $$ \frac{293}{3.00 \times 4.00} $$
Step 1: Identify Known and Unknown Variables
$$ \begin{aligned} V_1 &= 4.00\text{ L} & V_2 &= 3.00\text{ L} \\ P_1 &= \text{constant} & P_2 &= \text{constant} \\ T_1 &= 293\text{ K} \quad \quad \quad \quad & T_2 &= \text{?} \end{aligned} $$
Step 2: Substitute Variables into Combined Gas Law
$$ \frac{4.00\text{ L}}{293\text{ K}} = \frac{3.00\text{ L}}{T_2} $$
Step 3: Rearrange to Solve for Unknown
$$ T_2 = \frac{(3.00\text{ L})(293\text{ K})}{4.00\text{ L}} $$

5. A sample of helium gas has a volume of 900. milliliters and a pressure of 2.50 atm at 298 K. What is the new pressure when the temperature is changed to 336 K and the volume is decreased to 450. milliliters?
(1) 0.177 atm
(3) 5.64 atm
(2) 4.43 atm
(4) 14.1 atm
Step 1: Identify Known and Unknown Variables
$$ \begin{aligned} V_1 &= 900.\text{ mL} & V_2 &= 450.\text{ mL} \\ P_1 &= 2.50\text{ atm} & P_2 &= \text{?} \\ T_1 &= 298\text{ K} \quad \quad \quad \quad & T_2 &= 336\text{ K} \end{aligned} $$
Step 2: Substitute Variables into Combined Gas Law
$$ \frac{(2.50\text{ atm})(900.\text{ mL})}{298\text{ K}} = \frac{(P_2)(450.\text{ mL})}{336\text{ K}} $$
Step 3: Rearrange to Solve for Unknown
$$ P_2 = \frac{(2.50\text{ atm})(900.\text{ mL})(336\text{ K})}{(298\text{ K})(450.\text{ mL})} $$
Step 4: Final Calculated Result
$$ P_2 = 5.64\text{ atm} $$
6. A rigid cylinder with a moveable piston contains a 2.0-liter sample of neon gas at STP. What is the volume of this sample when its temperature is increased to $30.^\circ\text{C}$ while its pressure is decreased to 90. kilopascals?
(1) 2.5 L
(3) 1.6 L
(2) 2.0 L
(4) 0.22 L
Step 1: Identify Known and Unknown Variables (Convert STP and $^\circ\text{C}$)
$$ \begin{aligned} V_1 &= 2.0\text{ L} & V_2 &= \text{?} \\ P_1 &= 101.3\text{ kPa (STP)} & P_2 &= 90.\text{ kPa} \\ T_1 &= 273\text{ K (STP)} \quad \quad \quad \quad & T_2 &= 303\text{ K } (30^\circ\text{C} + 273) \end{aligned} $$
Step 2: Substitute Variables into Combined Gas Law
$$ \frac{(101.3\text{ kPa})(2.0\text{ L})}{273\text{ K}} = \frac{(90.\text{ kPa})(V_2)}{303\text{ K}} $$
Step 3: Rearrange to Solve for Unknown
$$ V_2 = \frac{(101.3\text{ kPa})(2.0\text{ L})(303\text{ K})}{(273\text{ K})(90.\text{ kPa})} $$
Step 4: Final Calculated Result
$$ V_2 = 2.5\text{ L} $$

$$ V_2 = \frac{(101.3\text{ kPa})(20.0\text{ mL})(283\text{ K})}{(297\text{ K})(94.6\text{ kPa})} $$

$$ V_2 = 20.4\text{ mL} $$
8. When the air bag inflates, the nitrogen gas is at a pressure of 1.30 atmospheres, a temperature of 301 K, and has a volume of 40.0 liters. In the space below, calculate the volume of the nitrogen gas at STP. Your response must include both a correct numerical setup and the calculated volume.
Step 1: Identify Known and Unknown Variables (Convert STP)
$$ \begin{aligned} V_1 &= 40.0\text{ L} & V_2 &= \text{?} \\ P_1 &= 1.30\text{ atm} & P_2 &= 1.00\text{ atm (STP)} \\ T_1 &= 301\text{ K} \quad \quad \quad \quad & T_2 &= 273\text{ K (STP)} \end{aligned} $$
Step 2: Substitute Variables into Combined Gas Law
$$ \frac{(1.30\text{ atm})(40.0\text{ L})}{301\text{ K}} = \frac{(1.00\text{ atm})(V_2)}{273\text{ K}} $$
Step 3: Rearrange to Solve for Unknown
$$ V_2 = \frac{(1.30\text{ atm})(40.0\text{ L})(273\text{ K})}{(301\text{ K})(1.00\text{ atm})} $$
Step 4: Final Calculated Result
$$ V_2 = 47.2\text{ L} $$
