Step 1 of 14

Chicken Riggies Prep

📋 View Shopping List
Mise En Place
Gather Basic Cooking Tools
Get out your primary measurement tools and base fat options:
  • Get out a tablespoon measuring spoon.
  • Get out standard measuring cups (1 cup, 1/2 cup).
  • Get out the EVOO (Extra Virgin Olive Oil).
  • Get out the butter container.
Mise En Place
Setup Rigatoni Water
Get out the rigatoni box and a large pot.
  • Fill the pot with water.
  • Place it on the stove.
  • Turn on the high heat to start boiling the water for it. We will drop the pasta in later in the sequence.
Mise En Place
Chop Aromatics & Sweet Peppers
Get your cutting board and chef's knife ready. Chop the following before continuing:
  • Get 1 onion and chop it up completely (diced).
  • Get 2 cubanelle peppers, seed them, and thinly slice them.
Mise En Place
Chop Spicy Peppers & Mushrooms
Finish your vegetable chopping cycle so you don't feel rushed later:
  • Get 8 oz of mushrooms and slice them up.
  • Get 3 Sorrento peppers, seed them, and dice them.
⚠️ WEAR GLOVES while cutting the Sorrento peppers!
Mise En Place
Prep & Cut the Chicken
Now handle the protein raw prep safely:
  • Get out 24 oz of chicken breast.
  • Cut it up into bite-sized pieces.
  • Place pieces into a clean prep bowl and set aside. Wash hands thoroughly.
Mise En Place
Final Measured Alignment
Measure out remaining assets so cooking is smooth:
  • Have the 28 oz can of crushed tomatoes opened and completely ready.
  • Measure out 1 cup of heavy cream.
  • Measure out 1/2 cup of parmesan cheese.
  • Measure out 1 tablespoon of minced garlic in EVOO.
  • Have 1 tablespoon of Italian seasoning ready.
Cooking
Heat Cast Iron Skillet
Get ready to brown the chicken. Everything is now fully prepped.
  • Get out your 12-inch cast iron skillet and place on medium-high heat.
  • Add 3 tablespoons of EVOO to the pan and let it get hot.
Cooking
Brown the Chicken Pieces
Carefully cook your prepped meat block:
  • Add the bite-sized chicken pieces into the hot oil.
  • Cook until all sides are lightly browned and no longer pink in the center.
  • Season generously with Salt and Pepper to taste while searing.
Cooking / Timing Focus
Transfer Chicken & Drop Pasta
Perfect workflow timing sequence checkpoint:
  • Remove chicken from the cast iron skillet and place into a bowl to keep warm.
  • Check your pasta pot on the other burner. It should be boiling intensely.
  • Drop the 1 lb package of rigatoni pasta into the boiling water now. Set a timer based on package instructions (usually 10-12 mins) to sync perfectly with the sauce completion!
Cooking
Sauté Onions & Cubanelles
Build deep flavor profiles inside the remaining skillet juices:
  • Add your prepped diced onions and sliced cubanelle peppers into the skillet.
  • Cook for approximately 5 minutes.
  • They should become soft but still hold a minor structural "slight bite".
Cooking
Sauté Mushrooms & Sorrentos
Incorporate remaining dynamic visual components:
  • Add the sliced mushrooms and diced Sorrento peppers to the skillet.
  • Add a generous pat of butter to enrich the pan.
  • Cook until elements are somewhat softer but remain structurally intact (not mushy).
Cooking
Aromatic Core Addition
Do not burn the garlic; manage space:
  • Clear a distinct empty circular space directly in the center of your pan.
  • Add your 1 tablespoon of minced garlic in EVOO into that center circle.
  • Cook for approximately one minute until deeply fragrant, then stir everything together.
Cooking
Combine Sauce Elements
Bring the structural sauce core to life:
  • Stir in the 28 oz can of crushed tomatoes.
  • Return the cooked chicken pieces (and any collected juices) back to the skillet.
  • Pour in the 1 cup of pre-measured heavy cream.
  • Add 1 tablespoon of Italian seasoning.
  • Taste the matrix and add more Italian seasoning if your palate desires.
  • Simmer gently for 2 to 3 minutes.
Ready to Eat
Final Integration
Unite the components for serving:
  • Drain your cooked rigatoni pasta.
  • Stir the rich hot sauce evenly into the rigatoni pasta.
  • Add the 1/2 cup of parmesan cheese and fold thoroughly. Serve hot!
